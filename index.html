Brake fault finder

Brake does not stop well

  • Worn linings: renew the shoes.
  • Dirt on the lining surface: clean it.
  • Arm fitted on the wrong cam spline: refit it to the punch marks.
  • Worn cam: renew it.

Bike feels slow, brakes binding

  • Brake adjusted too tight: a warm drum after a ride is the best evidence. Re-adjust the free play.

Brake grabs when applied gently

  • Shoe ends not chamfered: chamfer them with a file.
  • Oval drum: skim it lightly on a lathe.

Brake slow or spongy to release

  • Cam binding in its housing: free it and grease it.
  • Weak shoe springs: renew them.

Source: Haynes manual no. 572, chapter 5, fault diagnosis.
