## Figures already in the model

- Dimensions: length 1,990 mm, width 845 mm, height 1,105 mm, wheelbase 1,280 mm, seat height 744 mm,
  ground clearance 150 mm, dry weight 122 kg.
- Tyres 3.00-17 front (4PR) and rear (6PR); 17-inch chromed steel rims with steel spokes.
- Tyre pressures (up to 90 kg load): front 25 psi, rear 28 psi (maintenance) / 32 psi (specs);
  over 90 kg: rear 40 psi.
- Brakes: single leading shoe drums, front 140 mm (limit 141 mm), rear 130 mm (limit 131 mm);
  linings 4.0 mm new, 2.0 mm service limit.
- Free play: front lever end 10–20 mm; rear pedal 20–30 mm; stop lamp switch at about 20 mm of
  pedal travel. Wear indicator: arrow on the cam tag reaching the plate mark means renew the shoes.
- Fork: oil-damped telescopic, travel 115 mm, 135–140 cc ATF per leg, stanchion OD 30.915–30.950 mm.
- Rear suspension: travel 64 mm, spring free length 182.6 mm (limit 180.0 mm).
- Engine: 194 cc air-cooled OHC parallel twin, bore 53 mm, stroke 44 mm, 4-speed constant mesh.
- Torques: swinging arm pivot bolt 4.0–5.0 kgf m; wheel spindle nuts 4.0–5.0 kgf m.
- Electrical: the maintenance section describes a 6 V 12 Ah lead-acid battery. Check this against the
  owner's bike and the wiring diagram before stage 5; some CD200 variants are 12 V.
