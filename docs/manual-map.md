## Figures already in the model

- Dimensions: length 1,990 mm, width 845 mm, height 1,105 mm, wheelbase 1,280 mm, seat height 744 mm,
  ground clearance 150 mm, dry weight 122 kg.
- Tyres 3.00-17 front (4PR) and rear (6PR); 17-inch chromed steel rims with steel spokes.
- Tyre pressures (up to 90 kg load): front 25 psi, rear 28 psi (maintenance) / 32 psi (specs);
  over 90 kg: rear 40 psi.
- Brakes: single leading shoe drums, front 140 mm (limit 141 mm), rear 130 mm (limit 131 mm);
  linings 4.0 mm new, 2.0 mm service limit.
- Free play: front lever end 10–20 mm; rear pedal 20–30 mm; stop lamp switch at about 20 mm of
  pedal travel. Wear indicator: arrow on the cam tag reaching the plate mark means renew the shoes.
- Fork: oil-damped telescopic, travel 115 mm, 135–140 cc ATF per leg, stanchion OD 30.915–30.950 mm.
- Rear suspension: travel 64 mm, spring free length 182.6 mm (limit 180.0 mm).
- Engine: 194 cc air-cooled OHC parallel twin, bore 53 mm, stroke 44 mm, 4-speed constant mesh.
- Engine internals (specs p.24–25, CD200 column): bore 53.00–53.01 mm (limit 53.10), compression
  8.8:1; piston skirt 52.96–52.99 mm (limit 52.87); ring end gap 0.15–0.35 mm (limit 0.5); ring-to-
  groove 0.015–0.045 mm (limit 0.12); gudgeon pin 13.994–14.000 mm (limit 13.98); valve stem IN
  5.450–5.465 / EX 5.430–5.445 mm (limits 5.42 / 5.40); seat width 1.0 mm (limit 1.5), seat angle 45°;
  valve springs outer 36.45 mm (limit 35.5), inner 29.90 mm (limit 29.0); valve clearance cold
  0.05 mm IN and EX; cam lobe height IN 27.695 / EX 27.522 mm (limits 26.6 / 26.5); camshaft journal
  19.967–19.980 mm (limit 19.92), runout 0.05 mm; rocker bore 10.000–10.015 mm (limit 10.1);
  crankshaft runout 0.1 mm; small end 14.016–14.034 mm (limit 14.08); big-end axial 0.10–0.40 mm
  (limit 0.60), radial 0.004–0.012 mm (limit 0.05).
- Valve timing: the CD185T/CD200T column is blank (p.24–25). The model uses the CM200T column:
  inlet opens 7°30' BTDC, closes 27°30' ABDC; exhaust opens 27°30' BBDC, closes 7°30' ATDC.
- Valve clearance procedure (routine maintenance, p.13–14): T mark on the ATU to the fixed index mark,
  rockers free = TDC on compression, 0.05 mm feeler gauge, square-headed adjuster and locknut, one
  full turn (360°) for the other cylinder; camshaft slot at 3 o'clock; head cover bolts 0.8–1.2 kgf m.
- Ignition (chapter 3, p.90–94): one coil, one contact breaker, single F mark; 15° BTDC at 1,200 rpm,
  full advance 32–35° BTDC at 2,800 rpm (200 models); plugs CR7HS / U22FSR-L (UK), C7HS / U22FS (US),
  gap 0.6–0.7 mm.
- Engine fault diagnosis: PDF p.73–74 (printed 72–73).
- Gearbox (specs p.25, CD200 column): 4-speed constant mesh; primary reduction 3.632 : 1; gears 1st
  2.846, 2nd 1.778, 3rd 1.273, 4th 1.000 : 1; final reduction 2.333 : 1 ("all others"). Selector fork
  end 5.00–5.07 mm (limit 4.70); fork bore 12.000–12.018 mm (limit 12.05); fork shaft 11.976–11.994 mm
  (limit 11.96).
- Gear tooth counts (assembly photos 33.4a–m, p.47–49): mainshaft 1st 13T (part of the shaft), 2nd
  18T, 3rd 22T, 4th 22T; layshaft 1st 37T, 2nd 32T, 3rd 28T, 4th 22T. Primary drive gear 19T on the
  200 (p.46). Gearbox parts drawing fig 1.7 (p.49).
- Clutch (specs p.25): wet multiplate, 5 friction plates, 4 plain, 4 springs; friction plate 3.0 mm
  (limit 2.6); plain plate warpage 0.2 mm; spring free length 37.8 mm (limit 34.7) on the 200.
  Dismantling p.34 (thrust plate, springs, centre, plates, pressure plate); examination p.45; drawing
  fig 1.11 p.59. Release: cam on a vertical spindle in the right-hand cover, pushrod.
- Clutch adjustment (routine maintenance p.16): free play 10–20 mm at the lever end; main adjuster at
  the operating arm on the right-hand crankcase cover, fine adjuster at the lever.
- Final drive chain (p.11–12): about 20 mm play on the enclosed CD chain; sleeve nut 6.0–7.0 kgf m,
  spindle nut 4.0–5.0 kgf m.
- Clutch and gearbox fault diagnosis: PDF p.74–75 (printed 73–74).
- Torques: swinging arm pivot bolt 4.0–5.0 kgf m; wheel spindle nuts 4.0–5.0 kgf m.
- Electrical: the manual's models are 6 V (Yuasa 6N12A-2C, 12 Ah). **The owner's bike is 12 V**, so
  the manual's voltage-dependent figures are shown only as "manual (6 V models)".
- Electrical system (chapter 6, specs p.129–130): negative earth; fuse 20 A, spare in the right side
  panel (p.133); alternator 8-coil stator on the 200 (Hitachi); combined regulator/rectifier behind the
  right frame side panel, to the rear of the battery (p.131); starter motor at the front of the
  crankcase, left side, driving through a chain and one-way clutch behind the alternator (p.134);
  starter brushes 12.2–12.4 mm (limit 7.0), mica depth 0.5–0.8 mm (limit 0.2); starter solenoid
  function p.134; battery SG 1.260–1.280, charge at one tenth of capacity (p.131, 133). Bulbs (6 V):
  head 35/35 W, tail/stop 5/21 W, indicators 21 W, warning lamps 3 W / 1.7 W (p.130). Alternator
  drawing fig 6.2 (p.132). Wiring diagram "CD and CM 200 T" p.144 (too small to read the wire
  colours). Electrical fault diagnosis p.141 (printed 140).
- Ignition (chapter 3): contact breaker gap 0.3–0.4 mm; condenser 0.22–0.26 µF; ignition fault
  diagnosis p.96 (printed 95).
